# PageQuay 1.2.1

[Français](1.2.1.md) | [English](1.2.1.en.md)

20 October 2026 release: bounded recovery behind DOM layout envelopes.

## Depth correction

Structured extraction can recover a useful image behind nested layout containers.
Counted depth remains 32, with at most three neutral-envelope credits per path
and an absolute physical depth of 35, starting at body=0. Only `div`, `span`, and
`picture` elements satisfying the conservative classification in the
[contract](../EXTRACTION.en.md) receive credit. Envelopes stay in the returned tree.

Roles, ARIA attributes, labels, and language or interaction attributes prevent
credit; open Shadow hosts and assigned elements are excluded. Other limits stay
unchanged: 8000 visited nodes, shared text budget min(160000, max_content_bytes/8),
10 seconds per document and 40 seconds overall. Every cut retains `depth`,
`truncated`, and `partial`. XPath provenance keeps its separate 32-segment bound
and may report `depth_limit` without content loss. Diagnostics are never cleared
to force export acceptance.

On load, the local log records version, schema, and the SHA-256 fingerprint of
the script actually loaded. That line adds no secrets or page content. Exact
URLs, network controls, and structured schema 1.0 are preserved.

## Validation

- General suite: 347 passed, 145 skipped; browser tests run separately.
- Targeted Chromium: 29 passed, including 22 cases for depth, envelopes, images,
  siblings, Shadow/slot boundaries, privacy, and quotas.
- Ruff, formatting, public OpenAPI, and offline lockfile checks pass.
- Reading `https://www.lahakx.com/` in an isolated, protected temporary instance
  recovers `agri.png`: 16 images instead of 15; 1202 visited and 754 included nodes
  instead of 1199 and 750. Blocks, links, controls, forms, and tables are unchanged.
  The corrected snapshot has no reported omission.
- Local service installed and restarted: readiness and admin/API boundaries
  checked, exact OpenAPI 1.2.1 and loaded-script fingerprint verified; persistent
  settings and other distribution versions preserved.

Envelope classification remains conservative and heuristic. Deeper pages may
still be partial. The pre-existing Starlette/httpx deprecation remains.
UniversRobot is unchanged; its validation is not claimed as tested by this release.

## Included since the previous public release

The previous GitHub release was 1.1.0. This publication also includes local commits
[1.1.1](1.1.1.en.md) (transport without internal Base64) and
[1.2.0](1.2.0.en.md) (opt-in GIF and originals up to 20 MB), with their contracts,
limits, and historical validation. Dependencies remain locked.

Release assets are a wheel, source archive, and `SHA256SUMS`, built from the
published commit. Private service data stays outside packages and the repository.
